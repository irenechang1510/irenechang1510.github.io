Research

MEDAL

Manifold Embedding Distillation via Autoencoder Learning

MEDAL brings held-out validation to dimension reduction by distilling fitted embeddings into reusable encoder–decoder models. The resulting reconstruction error enables quantitative method comparison, hyperparameter tuning, distribution shift detection, and discovery of biological structure distorted by low-dimensional projections.

PaperCode

MEDALLION

Manifold Embedding Distillation and Attributions for Low-dimensional Latent InterpretatiON

Combining knowledge distillation and neural feature attribution to interpret embeddings from methods such as t-SNE and UMAP, with applications to identifying marker genes and unreliable structures in single-cell data.

Paper

Adaptive Multi-source Data Collection under Distribution Shift

Studying how to allocate samples across heterogeneous data sources to reduce uncertainty in a target domain. This work connects Bayesian sequential decision-making with optimal allocation and uses tabular foundation models to simulate future observations.

Self-supervised Learning for Whole-brain Neural Activity

2026–present

Developing a self-supervised Transformer for interventional whole-brain C. elegans calcium imaging, using multi-masking to predict cross-neuron interactions and explore causal relationships.
